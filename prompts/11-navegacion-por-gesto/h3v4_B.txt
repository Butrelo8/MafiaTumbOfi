One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera inside a small colonial Mexican sacristy at night: a dark carved chest of drawers with a vinyl record turntable on top, a warm table lamp, a religious painting, a thin red neon tube at the right edge. The camera drifts very slowly. The black vinyl record spins slowly and steadily on the turntable. The lamp glows steadily, the red neon hums with a faint flicker. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
