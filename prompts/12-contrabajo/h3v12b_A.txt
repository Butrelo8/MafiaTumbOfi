One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera inside an old colonial Mexican church nave at night: stone piers, rows of dark wooden pews, gilded altarpiece with saint statues, a red neon cross above it. In the right foreground an old upright double bass with sloping shoulders, f-holes and a carved scroll, leans against the first pew, and a small acoustic guitar stands on the pew with its headstock resting against the scroll of the bass, like offerings, among small votive candles on the floor. The instruments stay perfectly still, solid and unchanged, their strings straight and taut. The camera drifts very slowly. The candle flames on the altar and the votive candles flicker gently. A thin, even haze of copal incense smoke drifts slowly and steadily to the left through the warm candlelight, always the same density. The red neon cross glows steadily. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
