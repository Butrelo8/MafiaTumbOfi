One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera inside an old colonial Mexican church nave at night: stone piers, rows of dark wooden pews, gilded altarpiece with saint statues, a red neon cross above it. In the right foreground an old upright double bass with sloping shoulders, f-holes and a carved scroll, leans against the first pew, and to its left on the pew seat a small acoustic guitar leans back against a black portable loudspeaker, like offerings. On the loudspeaker a small clay incense burner with glowing copal and a lit votive candle; more votive candles on the floor and on the second pew. The instruments stay perfectly still, solid and unchanged, their strings straight and taut. The camera drifts very slowly. The candle flames on the altar and the votive candles flicker gently. A thin ribbon of copal smoke rises steadily from the clay incense burner on the loudspeaker and drifts slowly to the left, always the same density; the votive candles give off only faint wisps. The red neon cross glows steadily. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
