One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera inside an old colonial Mexican church nave at night: stone piers, rows of dark wooden pews, gilded altarpiece with saint statues, a red neon cross above it. In the right foreground an old upright double bass leans against the first pew; on the pew seat a small acoustic guitar leans back against a black loudspeaker, among small votive candles. The instruments stay perfectly still, solid and unchanged, their strings straight and taut. The camera trucks smoothly to the right past the instruments and passes close behind a dark stone pier that fills the whole frame, turning the image black. The lighting never changes: the stone floor stays dark and in shadow, the moonlight stays in its shafts. The candle flames flicker gently and thin copal incense smoke drifts slowly through the warm candlelight. The red neon cross glows steadily. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
