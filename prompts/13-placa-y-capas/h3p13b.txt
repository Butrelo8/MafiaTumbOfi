One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera locked off on a tripod, perfectly still, inside an old colonial Mexican church nave at night: stone piers, rows of dark wooden pews, gilded carved altarpiece with saint statues, a red neon cross above it. In the right foreground an old upright double bass leans against the first pew, and on the pew seat a small acoustic guitar leans back against a black portable loudspeaker. On the loudspeaker a small clay incense burner with glowing copal and a lit votive candle; more votive candles on the pews. Everything stays perfectly still and unchanged: the carved altarpiece, the saints, the instruments and their strings. Only the candle flames flicker gently. A thin ribbon of copal smoke rises steadily from the clay incense burner, always the same density; the votive candles give off only faint wisps. The red neon cross glows steadily. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
