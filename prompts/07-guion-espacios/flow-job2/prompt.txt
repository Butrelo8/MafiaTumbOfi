One continuous shot. The camera moves only sideways, slowly to the right: no push-in, no zoom. The dark stage jackets in the open wardrobe pass right against the lens and cover the entire frame, so the image is completely black for about half a second. When the black clears, the camera is already in the stone crypt of the last frame and comes to rest. Warm candlelight in the first room, the cold moonbeam in the crypt. No new objects, no clothing racks, no new lights or lamps, no lens flare, no cyan, no people, no text.
