One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera locked off on a tripod, perfectly still, vertical frame, at night on a cobblestone street in Xalapa, Mexico, in front of an old colonial stone church facade: the arched wooden portal is open with warm candlelight inside and a small lit candelabra, a black wrought-iron gate, the back of a black pickup truck with its red tail lights on, wet cobblestones in the foreground. Everything stays perfectly still and unchanged: the facade, the portal, the gate, the truck and the stones. Only a thin layer of low ground fog drifts very slowly across the wet cobblestones, close to the ground, always the same density, with no gusts, catching faint red and warm light; the candelabra flames inside the portal sway softly, never jumping or flaring. Nothing hurries. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
