One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera locked off on a tripod, perfectly still, vertical frame, inside a low vaulted crypt under an old colonial Mexican church at night: rough dark volcanic stone arches and burial niches, a stone altar with an old gold rotary telephone between two lit votive candles in red glass, a narrow shaft of cold silver moonlight falling from a small iron grate in the vault onto the altar. Everything stays perfectly still and unchanged: the stone, the altar, the gold telephone and its coiled cord, the shape and brightness of the moonbeam. Only fine dust motes drift slowly inside the moonbeam, floating and turning lazily, always the same amount, and the two candle flames sway softly, never jumping or flaring. Nothing hurries. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
