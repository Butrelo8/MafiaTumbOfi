One continuous shot, no cuts. Real footage filmed on a cinema camera locked off on a tripod, perfectly still, at night on a cobblestone street in Xalapa, Mexico, in front of an old colonial stone church facade: the arched wooden portal is open with warm candlelight inside and a small lit candelabra, a black wrought-iron gate, a black pickup truck parked on the right with its red tail lights on, a thin street lamp pole with an orange sodium lamp, misty mountains behind. Everything stays perfectly still and unchanged: the facade, the portal, the gate, the truck, the lamp pole and the wet cobblestones. Only thin mountain fog drifts very slowly across the dark sky and the mountains behind the lamp, always the same density, with no gusts; the sodium lamp hums with a faint, steady glow, and the candelabra flames inside the portal sway softly, never jumping or flaring. Nothing hurries. Low key, deep black shadows, fine film grain. Nothing else moves. Audio is ambient sound only: quiet room tone.
