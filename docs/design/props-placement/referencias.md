# Props and placement: real references (2026-11-13, run `touchgrasdrone`)

Brief: mothership `docs/queue/2026-11-13-mto-props-placement.md`. Earlier passes (`docs/design/references.md` › Scene props,
Stickers, Bed props) had no opened photo of a decorated truck: every real-world row there is "S" (search results only). This
pass adds photos that were downloaded and looked at.

**Marks.** **O** = the photo itself was downloaded and looked at (at 640 px or the page's lead image). **T** = text only: the
page or a search excerpt was read, no photo was seen; these do not count toward the 15. Photos are linked, not copied: the repo is
public and the news photos are not ours. Commons files are CC-licensed; reuse needs the attribution on their file page.

**The gap, plainly.** No photo of a decorated Mexican band, norteño or corridos tumbados tour truck was found (resa, WebSearch,
Wikimedia Commons). The nearest are a mariachi band riding a decorated wedding pickup (#14) and a US band beside a plain tour van
with no stickers (#15). Everything below about band trucks is therefore borrowed from neighbours: carnival floats, pilgrimage and
fiesta vehicles, lighted truck parades, Mexican pickups and dashboards, and the one aftermarket part made for our exact truck.
