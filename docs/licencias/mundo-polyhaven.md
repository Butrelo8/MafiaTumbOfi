# The world around the truck (Night B)

Every asset `blender/mundo.py` loads is from Poly Haven and is CC0 (public domain, no attribution required):
https://polyhaven.com/license. They were downloaded on 2026-11-14 and are kept outside the repo in
`~/cc-out/mto-world/assets/polyhaven-2026-11-14/` (`mundo.ASSETS_MUNDO`), at 1k for textures and models and 2k for the HDRI.

- Plants (glTF): grass_medium_02, weed_plant_02, shrub_02, shrub_04, pachira_aquatica_01, fern_02
- Surfaces (JPG): asphalt_02, gravelly_sand, sparse_grass
- Sky (HDR): kloppenheim_07_puresky

The far tree crowns, the markings, the studs, the CFE poles and the wires are built by the script, with no third-party mesh.
No paid asset is used.
